Docs & Help

Everything you need to get the most out of ClipKings.

Quick Start

1
Upload or paste — Use Clip Studio for a single URL/file, or drag files straight into the Video Editor timeline.
2
Arrange clips — Drag clips on the timeline to reorder. Drag the edge handles to trim start/end. Use zoom (+ / −) to see fine detail.
3
Pick a layout — Choose crop, split-screen, or a custom template from the Templates page. The Editor lets you render free-form.
4
Render — Click Render Sequence (Editor) or Generate (Clip Studio / Ranking). FFmpeg runs locally — no cloud, no watermarks.
5
Download — Your export appears immediately. Grab it from the Exports page any time within 30 days.

Video Editor

The Editor is a full multi-track timeline. Three tracks run in parallel: Photo, Video, and Audio. Click the track icon on the left of the timeline to add media to that track.

ElementWhat it does
Timeline clipClick to select. Drag left/right edge to trim. Proportional width shows true duration.
PlayheadVertical orange bar. Click anywhere on the timeline ruler to seek.
Zoom (+ / −)Makes clips wider/narrower so you can see fine trim detail.
Properties panelShows start, end, duration, volume for the selected clip. Drag its left edge to resize.
FilmstripVideo clips show auto-generated frame thumbnails rendered in the browser.
Resize handlesDrag the bar above the timeline up/down to change its height. Drag the properties panel edge left/right.

Render settings: choose resolution (9:16, 16:9, square), codec (H.264 / H.265 / VP9), and fill mode (crop or letterbox).

Clip Studio

The fastest way to go from a raw video to a ready-to-post short. Paste a URL (YouTube, TikTok, Twitter, Reddit, etc.) or upload a local file. Set in/out points on the timeline slider, pick your template, and hit Generate.

The background URL field (for split-screen) is optional. Leave it blank to use a solid background or the center-crop template.

Templates

Ranking

Create a numbered countdown video automatically. Add up to 20 items, each with a title label and a video clip. The Ranking tool stitches them together, overlays the rank number and label, and renders a complete countdown video.

Customize: keyword text and color, subtitle, font size, output resolution, and codec. Mute toggle strips audio for a clean background-music-ready output.

AI Studio

AI Studio combines two local AI models to enhance your videos without any external API:

  • Whisper — OpenAI's speech-recognition model transcribes your video's audio and burns word-level subtitles directly onto the frame. Runs entirely on your machine.
  • edge-tts — Microsoft's neural text-to-speech engine generates a high-quality voiceover from a script you provide. Choose from dozens of voices and languages. Also runs locally.

You can use subtitles-only, voiceover-only, or both at the same time. The output is a single rendered MP4 ready to post.

Exports

Every render lands in your Exports hub. From there you can preview, download (MP4/WebM), or delete individual files. Exports are stored for up to 30 days then automatically purged — download anything you want to keep.

The Exports page includes search and filters so you can find old renders quickly if you have many.

FAQ

Why does rendering take a while?
FFmpeg re-encodes your video entirely on your server's CPU. Longer clips, higher resolutions (1080p vs 720p), or slower hardware will take longer. H.265 is smaller but slower to encode than H.264.
Can I use videos I don't own?
You are responsible for having the rights to any content you process. See our Terms of Service for details.
Where are my exports stored?
Exports are stored on the server where ClipKings is running, in your account's exports directory. They are accessible only to you. Files are deleted after 30 days.
Does AI Studio need an internet connection?
Whisper and edge-tts run locally on the server — no internet required after the models are downloaded on first use. No data is sent to third parties.
What video formats are supported?
Input: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, M4V, TS, FLV, and most formats FFmpeg can read. Output: MP4 (H.264 / H.265) or WebM (VP9).
