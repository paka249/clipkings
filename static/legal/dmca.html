DMCA & Copyright Policy

Short-Form Studio  ·  Effective date: June 9, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 15, 2025

Short-Form Studio respects intellectual property rights. This policy describes how we handle copyright infringement claims under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), 17 U.S.C. § 512, and our obligations as a service provider that stores user-generated content — including rendered video exports, multi-track editor outputs, ranking videos, and AI-processed media — in the Exports hub.

1. DMCA Safe Harbor — Section 512

Short-Form Studio operates as an online service provider under the safe harbor provisions of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), 17 U.S.C. § 512(c). We store user-generated processed video content in the "Exports" hub on behalf of our users. This includes:

  • Videos rendered by the Video Editor (multi-track timeline compositions of photo, video, and audio clips)
  • Videos processed by Clip Studio (trimmed and cropped shorts from URLs or uploaded files)
  • Videos generated by the Ranking tool (automated countdown videos)
  • Videos processed by AI Studio (Whisper subtitles and/or edge-tts voiceover overlaid on user-supplied video)
  • Videos rendered using any Template (center-crop, split-screen, gaming background)

To qualify for safe harbor protection, we:

  • Do not have actual knowledge of infringement or, upon obtaining such knowledge, act expeditiously to remove or disable access to infringing material
  • Do not receive a direct financial benefit from infringing activity where we have the right and ability to control such activity
  • Upon proper notification, act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the claimed infringing material
  • Designate a Copyright Agent (see Section 2 below) and have registered that agent with the U.S. Copyright Office
  • Maintain and enforce a Repeat Infringer Policy (see Section 6 below)

3. Scope of DMCA Claims

The DMCA notice process applies to content stored in Short-Form Studio's Exports hub. This includes:

  • Finished export files containing copyrighted video, audio, or images the user did not have rights to process
  • Exports where AI-generated voiceover (edge-tts) is overlaid on copyrighted source material without authorization
  • Exports where Whisper-generated subtitles are burned into copyrighted video without authorization
  • Ranking videos that incorporate copyrighted clips without authorization
  • Multi-track editor exports that combine copyrighted media without authorization

The DMCA process does not apply to the AI models themselves (Whisper, edge-tts) or the editor/rendering software — those are open-source tools running on the server.

4. Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you believe that content stored in Short-Form Studio's Exports hub infringes your copyright, you may submit a written takedown notice to our Copyright Agent. To be valid under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your notice must include all of the following elements:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work: A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed, or if multiple works are covered, a representative list.
  2. Identification of the infringing material: A description of the material that you claim is infringing and that is to be removed, with sufficient detail to permit us to locate it (e.g., the specific filename or URL of the exported video in the Exports hub).
  3. Contact information: Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address so we can contact you.
  4. Good faith statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. Accuracy statement: A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  6. Signature: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

Warning: Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing may be subject to liability for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees, incurred by the alleged infringer or the service provider.

4.1 Response Process

Upon receipt of a valid, complete takedown notice, we will:

  • Acknowledge receipt within 2 business days
  • Remove or disable access to the identified material promptly (typically within 3 business days of verification)
  • Notify the user who uploaded/rendered the material that it has been removed
  • Provide the user with a copy of the takedown notice (with the complainant's contact information, as required by law)

5. Counter-Notice Procedure

If you believe that material was removed from the Exports hub as a result of a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written counter-notice to our Copyright Agent. A valid counter-notice under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3) must include:

  1. Identification of removed material: A description of the material that was removed and the location (URL or filename) at which it appeared before removal.
  2. Statement under penalty of perjury: A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material.
  3. Consent to jurisdiction: A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or if outside the United States, in any judicial district in which Short-Form Studio may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the complainant or their agent.
  4. Contact information: Your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  5. Signature: Your physical or electronic signature.

5.1 Restoration Process

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, we will:

  • Forward a copy of the counter-notice to the original complainant
  • Inform the complainant that we will restore the material in 10–14 business days unless the complainant notifies us that they have filed a court action seeking to restrain the user from the infringing activity
  • Restore the material (or provide access again) within 10–14 business days of receiving the counter-notice, unless the complainant files a court action

6. Non-DMCA Copyright Claims

For copyright concerns that do not involve content stored in our Exports hub (e.g., concerns about the Service itself, the editor software, or the AI model weights), please contact us at copyright@shortformstudio.com. We will review legitimate concerns on a case-by-case basis.

7. Repeat Infringer Policy

Mandatory Account Termination: Short-Form Studio maintains a strict Repeat Infringer Policy as required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(i). Any user who receives three (3) or more valid, uncontested DMCA takedown notices — or who is determined to have repeatedly submitted unauthorized copyrighted material for processing through any Service feature (Editor, Clip Studio, Ranking, AI Studio, or Templates) — will have their account permanently terminated without refund or appeal.

In determining whether a user is a repeat infringer, we consider:

  • The number of valid DMCA notices received involving the user's exports
  • Whether the user has submitted counter-notices that were ultimately found to be in bad faith
  • Whether the user has acknowledged (in account settings or communications) that they did not have rights to processed content
  • Patterns suggesting systematic infringement across multiple features (e.g., repeatedly submitting the same copyright owner's content through different tools)
  • Deliberate use of AI Studio (subtitles/voiceover) to add value to content obtained without authorization, indicating willful infringement

We reserve the right to terminate accounts with fewer than three notices if the infringement is particularly egregious, involves commercial-scale infringement, or involves deliberate circumvention of rights management systems.

7.1 Account Reinstatement

Accounts terminated under this policy are not eligible for reinstatement. A terminated user may not create a new account using the same email address, and we reserve the right to terminate accounts we determine belong to previously banned users.

8. Good Faith and Abuse

We take the DMCA process seriously and will not tolerate abuse of the takedown system. Filing fraudulent or bad-faith takedown notices is a violation of our Terms of Service and may expose the filer to legal liability under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f). We reserve the right to report suspected abuse to appropriate authorities.
